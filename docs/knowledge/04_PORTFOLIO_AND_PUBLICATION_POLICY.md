## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the public and professional presentation of the Officer-Bound Digital Investigation Agent project.

The project may serve simultaneously as:

1. an independent cybersecurity research and engineering project;
2. a security reference architecture;
3. a controlled proof of concept;
4. professional portfolio evidence.

These purposes do not create institutional endorsement, operational authority, production readiness or legal status. Portfolio objectives shall never override security, legality, ethics, privacy, technical accuracy or the governing documentation hierarchy.

This policy is subordinate to `01_CANONICAL_PROJECT_DEFINITION.md` and `MASTER_DOCUMENTATION_CONSTITUTION.md`.

## 2. Governing Principles

- **PUB-REQ-001:** Public presentation shall preserve human accountability and officer-bound delegation.
- **PUB-REQ-002:** The AI agent shall never be represented as possessing independent legal authority.
- **PUB-REQ-003:** Public claims shall distinguish conceptual, designed, implemented, tested, simulated, deferred and out-of-scope capabilities.
- **PUB-REQ-004:** No public artifact shall overstate validation, security, compliance, deployment status, institutional approval or professional experience.
- **PUB-REQ-005:** Publication shall use precise, restrained and technically defensible language.
- **PUB-REQ-006:** Public release shall occur only after the applicable security, privacy, documentation and release gates are completed.
- **PUB-REQ-007:** Public material shall not silently expand the project scope.
- **PUB-REQ-008:** Reviewer and recruiter readability shall not be achieved by removing material limitations or safety boundaries.

## 3. Approved Project Positioning

The approved project description is:

> An independent cybersecurity research and engineering project exploring a human-accountable architecture for officer-bound AI agents supporting authorized digital investigations.

The description may be adapted for length or audience only when the following remain explicit or unambiguous:

- the project is independent research and engineering;
- the architecture is human-accountable;
- the agent is officer-bound;
- support is limited to authorized digital investigations;
- the project is not represented as an operational law-enforcement platform;
- implementation and validation status are stated accurately.

## 4. Prohibited Project Representations

The project shall not be described as:

- an official police system;
- a deployed law-enforcement platform;
- a production-ready investigative tool unless formally demonstrated and approved under future authorized governance;
- a system approved or endorsed by public authorities without documentary evidence;
- an autonomous police agent;
- a system currently used in real investigations without verified authorization and evidence;
- an AI system with independent legal authority;
- the first project of its kind without authoritative evidence;
- fully compliant with law or standards solely because mappings exist;
- independently certified, audited or validated without corresponding evidence;
- secure, private, explainable or tamper-proof without defined scope and validation.

- **PUB-REQ-009:** Prohibited representations shall be removed before publication.
- **PUB-REQ-010:** Ambiguous wording that reasonably implies a prohibited representation shall be corrected.
- **PUB-REQ-011:** Historical public claims found to be inaccurate shall be corrected through a traceable publication update.

## 6. Recruiter and Reviewer Readability

A technical recruiter, hiring manager, reviewer or researcher should be able to identify efficiently:

- the problem addressed;
- the proposed solution;
- the human-accountability model;
- the officer-bound delegation model;
- the authorization and safety boundaries;
- the implemented and non-implemented components;
- the demonstration method;
- the validation status;
- the principal cybersecurity competencies evidenced;
- the project’s limitations.

- **PUB-REQ-016:** The top-level repository presentation shall not require readers to infer implementation status.
- **PUB-REQ-017:** Architectural depth may be layered through navigation, but critical limitations shall remain visible.
- **PUB-REQ-018:** Simplification for readability shall not alter normative meaning.

## 7. Implementation-Status Vocabulary

Every described feature or capability shall use one of the following approved states.

### 7.1 Conceptual

A documented idea or requirement not yet expressed as an approved architecture.

### 7.2 Designed

An approved or reviewable architecture or specification exists, but implementation is not complete.

### 7.3 Partially Implemented

Some required components exist, but the complete feature or control is not implemented.

### 7.4 Implemented

The required implementation exists in the stated environment, but this status alone does not establish validation.

### 7.5 Tested

Defined tests have been executed and retained evidence supports the stated result and scope.

### 7.6 Simulated

The capability is demonstrated through synthetic data, mock services, testnets or controlled environments rather than operational deployment.

### 7.7 Deferred

The capability is within approved scope but intentionally postponed with rationale and dependency record.

### 7.8 Out of Scope

The capability is not authorized or planned under the applicable baseline.

- **PUB-REQ-019:** Status labels shall be applied at the feature level where aggregate project status would be misleading.
- **PUB-REQ-020:** `Implemented` shall not be used as a synonym for `Tested`, `Validated`, `Secure` or `Production Ready`.
- **PUB-REQ-021:** Status changes shall be supported by traceable repository evidence.
- **PUB-REQ-022:** Screenshots, diagrams and demonstrations shall display or link to the applicable implementation status.

## 8. Repository Integrity

The repository shall not publish:

- empty normative files or deceptive placeholders;
- unsupported claims;
- fabricated metrics;
- fabricated citations, identifiers, approvals or validation;
- hidden operational capabilities;
- credentials, tokens, secrets or private keys;
- real personal data;
- real case information;
- unlawfully obtained datasets;
- stolen data or credentials;
- active exploitation functionality;
- malware;
- unsafe Dark Web interaction;
- uncontrolled criminal-infrastructure interaction;
- misleading screenshots or demonstrations;
- operational details whose publication creates unnecessary abuse potential.

- **PUB-REQ-023:** Secret scanning and manual disclosure review shall be completed before public release.
- **PUB-REQ-024:** Personal, case and sensitive operational data shall be excluded or irreversibly replaced by approved synthetic or fictitious data.
- **PUB-REQ-025:** Repository history shall be considered during disclosure review; deleting a secret from the current tree alone is insufficient.
- **PUB-REQ-026:** Unsafe or restricted material discovered after publication shall trigger containment, removal, credential revocation where applicable, incident documentation and release correction.

## 9. Safe Demonstrations

Public demonstrations shall use only:

- synthetic cases;
- fictitious identities;
- local infrastructure;
- mock APIs and services;
- read-only lawful public sources;
- testnets;
- controlled cybersecurity laboratories;
- authorized CTF environments;
- archived lawful and authorized datasets;
- simulated or operationally isolated Dark Web environments;
- controlled cloud environments without real investigative data.

- **PUB-REQ-027:** A demonstration shall document its data origin, environment, permissions, limitations and reset procedure.
- **PUB-REQ-028:** Demonstrations shall not interact with real criminal marketplaces or uncontrolled criminal infrastructure.
- **PUB-REQ-029:** Demonstrations shall not investigate real persons.
- **PUB-REQ-030:** Demonstration credentials shall be non-production, scoped, revocable and excluded from version control.
- **PUB-REQ-031:** A safe demonstration shall remain non-destructive and defensive unless an explicitly authorized controlled cybersecurity laboratory requires otherwise.
- **PUB-REQ-032:** A demonstration disclaimer shall not be used to excuse unsafe functionality.

## 10. Communication Style

Public language shall be precise, restrained and technically defensible.

Avoid unsupported or inflated expressions such as:

- revolutionary;
- unprecedented;
- military-grade;
- unhackable;
- perfect security;
- guaranteed compliance;
- fully autonomous;
- real-time global surveillance;
- first in the world.

- **PUB-REQ-033:** Superlatives require objective, current and reproducible evidence and shall remain bounded to the supported claim.
- **PUB-REQ-034:** Security properties shall identify their scope, assumptions, controls and validation status.
- **PUB-REQ-035:** Compliance language shall distinguish mapping, readiness, assessment, evidence and verified conformance.
- **PUB-REQ-036:** Legal and regulatory language shall follow `03_RESEARCH_AND_SOURCE_POLICY.md`.

## 11. Public Architecture and Responsible Disclosure

The public repository should emphasize:

- security architecture;
- defensive controls;
- threat models;
- governance;
- evidence integrity;
- safe proof-of-concept components;
- reproducible laboratories;
- limitations and residual risks.

- **PUB-REQ-037:** Operational details shall be withheld, generalized or access-controlled when publication creates material abuse risk without sufficient public benefit.
- **PUB-REQ-038:** Withholding details shall not be used to conceal material risk, misrepresent implementation or fabricate security.
- **PUB-REQ-039:** Vulnerability information shall follow coordinated and responsible disclosure practices appropriate to the affected party and risk.
- **PUB-REQ-040:** Public architecture shall preserve enough information for technical review without publishing unnecessary abuse-enabling instructions.

## 13. Publication and Release Gate

Before any public release, the Release Reviewer shall verify and retain evidence that:

- [ ] no secrets or credentials are present in the current tree or relevant history;
- [ ] no real personal data or case information is present;
- [ ] no unauthorized or unlawfully obtained material is present;
- [ ] no unsafe or prohibited functionality is present;
- [ ] no legal or institutional misrepresentation is present;
- [ ] no unsupported claim, metric, citation, approval or validation is present;
- [ ] documentation is complete for the release scope;
- [ ] execution or demonstration instructions are accurate where applicable;
- [ ] limitations and residual risks are visible;
- [ ] project and feature status labels are accurate;
- [ ] security review is complete;
- [ ] privacy and fundamental-rights review is complete;
- [ ] implementation-alignment review is complete;
- [ ] license and third-party attribution obligations are satisfied where applicable;
- [ ] release artifacts correspond to the reviewed commit;
- [ ] release approval is recorded.

- **PUB-REQ-044:** Failure of a mandatory publication check blocks public release.
- **PUB-REQ-045:** Private repository incorporation does not by itself constitute public release approval.
- **PUB-REQ-046:** A release shall identify the exact commit, version, evidence and approval record.
- **PUB-REQ-047:** Material post-release corrections shall be documented and versioned.

## 14. Publication Evidence

Publication evidence shall include, where applicable:

- release identifier;
- reviewed commit;
- release date;
- reviewer identity and role;
- document and implementation versions;
- security review result;
- privacy review result;
- secret and sensitive-data review result;
- validation status;
- known limitations;
- accepted residual risks;
- approval decision;
- supersession or withdrawal record.

- **PUB-REQ-048:** Publication evidence shall be retained according to the Constitution.
- **PUB-REQ-049:** Audit history shall be tamper-evident where technically feasible.
- **PUB-REQ-050:** A withdrawn release shall remain historically traceable unless legal or security containment requires restricted access.

- **PUB-REQ-054:** Unapproved publication-method changes during active project phases are prohibited.

## 17. Limitations

- This policy does not itself authorize public release.
- Private repository status does not eliminate security, privacy or disclosure risk.
- A publication review cannot guarantee that every future misuse or disclosure risk has been eliminated.
- Portfolio readability does not establish independent technical validation.
- Repository evidence does not establish professional employment experience.
- Publication of an architecture does not establish that it is implemented, secure, lawful or suitable for operational use.
- Jurisdiction-specific publication, data-protection, export, law-enforcement or cybersecurity obligations may require qualified review.
